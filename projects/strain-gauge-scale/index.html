THE SIGNAL CHAIN

Condition, measure, calibrate.

The load cell produced a small signal that needed amplification and offset before the Arduino ADC could read it. I designed the instrumentation and offset stages, verified gain in Simulink, then calibrated the MATLAB readout at two known loads.

DESIGN & VERIFICATION

Check the gain before building the circuit.

  • Derived the instrumentation-amplifier output using nodal analysis.
  • Modeled the circuit in Simulink and confirmed the expected output of 1.45 V and gain of 210 before hardware assembly.
  • Solved the offset-amplifier resistor values to scale the signal into the Arduino ADC range.
  • Implemented a 100 Hz MATLAB acquisition loop and calibrated the mass readout against 0 kg and 2 kg references.

RESULT

Validated the readout at two reference loads.

The measured gain was 210. The output spanned 0.62–2.41 V against a 0.5–2.5 V target range, and the mass readout was validated at 0 kg and 2 kg. The remaining difference from the target range was attributed to standard-value resistor substitutions.
