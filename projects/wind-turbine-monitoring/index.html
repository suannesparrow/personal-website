Wind turbine
health monitoring.

A low-cost structural-health-monitoring prototype for wind turbines in seismic-prone regions. I led embedded firmware for multi-sensor acquisition, reliable data handling, and network communication.

PROJECTUBC Capstone · 2025–2026MY ROLEEmbedded firmware developmentTEAM RESULTCapstone first place

THE CHALLENGE

Make continuous monitoring practical.

The goal was to prototype an affordable system that could collect vibration, inclination, and temperature data at a wind turbine, move it reliably over a wired network, and make it available for remote visualization.

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

From sensor to dashboard

I worked on the sensor-node and communications side of an end-to-end prototype. The system connected MEMS sensors to an ESP32, transmitted structured readings over Ethernet, and delivered them to local storage for dashboard use.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Keep acquisition deterministic; make failures visible.

  • Implemented register-level SPI drivers for the ADXL355Z accelerometer and SCL3300 inclinometer, plus an I²C driver for the ADT7420 temperature sensor.
  • Built timer- and interrupt-driven sampling with lock-free ring buffers, separating sensor acquisition from FreeRTOS processing and network transmission.
  • Brought up ESP32 Ethernet over PoE and implemented structured MQTT publishing for measurements, status, and fault events.
  • Added reconnect handling, dropped-sample logging, and NaN handling for partial sensor failures.

VALIDATION & OUTCOME

Tested end-to-end for a full day.

The prototype moved sensor readings through to Raspberry Pi storage and dashboard visualization. The team validated simultaneous seven-channel acquisition, fault propagation to the interface, and 24 hours of operation with 99.83% data integrity.

99.83% data integrity over 24 hours

WHAT I’D CARRY FORWARD

Reliability is an end-to-end property.

Reliable sampling depends on the full path: sensor timing, buffering, task scheduling, transport, and recovery behavior. Separating those stages made failures easier to identify without blocking acquisition.
